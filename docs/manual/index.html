1. What it is

HQ CuePlayer is a companion controller for HQPlayer Embedded. HQPlayer does not play CUE sheets natively — HQ CuePlayer fixes that, turning single-file FLAC/WAV rips (and NRG/ISO/BIN images) into a track-level, gapless, cross-album playlist experience. It also turns every Linux chore — deployment, NAS mounts, DSP tuning — into one-click operations.

What it is not: it is not a playback engine and does not include HQPlayer. All decoding, resampling and output are performed by your own licensed HQPlayer — the audio path is bit-identical to the official pipeline.

3. Step 1 — Deploy hqpd over SSH

The server side of HQ CuePlayer is hqpd, a tiny daemon living on the player. Deploy once, forget forever:

1
Open the SSH Deploy page. Enter the player IP (or use Scan to discover it) and the SSH credentials.
2
Click Deploy. It auto-detects the CPU architecture, uploads hqpd, registers a systemd service and self-checks — about 20 seconds, all green.
3
✓ API pairing token retrieved means secure pairing completed automatically. Nothing else to configure.
Fig. 1 — A successful deploy
About passwords: every field (including passwords) is remembered automatically and stored only in hqcueplayer.json on your own PC. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

4. Step 2 — Mount your NAS

This lets the player read music straight off your NAS — the foundation of instant, zero-upload playback.

1
On the NAS Settings page (left column), fill in the player SSH address, the SMB share (e.g. //192.168.1.26/music) and NAS credentials. Leave credentials blank for public shares — guest mode kicks in automatically.
2
Click Test to probe the SMB port first, then Mount NAS.
3
Keep Write fstab checked for boot-time auto-mount. Leave the protocol on Auto; only fall back to SMB 2.0/1.0 for ancient NAS boxes.
Fig. 2 — NAS Settings (left: mount; right: web settings, path mapping, license)
If mounting fails: the log tells you why (bad address, bad credentials, protocol mismatch). Anti-garbled-filename and anti-lockup parameters are built in, and if the NAS drops offline the player retries the mount every minute automatically.

5. Step 3 — Path mapping (zero upload)

Tell HQ CuePlayer "this Windows path equals that player path". From then on, dropping a CUE into the window makes the player read the original file straight off the NAS — multi-GB rips open instantly, not a single byte uploaded.

SettingExample
Windows prefix\\192.168.1.26\9394\music (as seen in Explorer; separate multiple prefixes with ;)
Linux prefix/mnt/nas (the mount point from Step 2)

Click Save Mapping. Hostname and IP are treated as the same host, so either spelling works.

The right panel is an audiophile's comfort zone: Signal Chain shows source format → filter → shaper → output in real time, and Engine Health shows load and the clipping counter. If Clips starts climbing, the DSP chain is over-amplified — pull the volume down 1–2 dB.

7. DSP settings & presets

Click DSP Settings… in the right panel: output mode (PCM / DSD), filter 1x/Nx, shaper and rate — all applied in real time.

Fig. 4 — DSP settings with presets and auto-match rules

Three power features

  • Learn All Filters — scans and names every filter/shaper ID in about a minute. Click once after an OS reinstall (or whenever names revert to bare IDs).
  • Scene presets — snapshot the entire chain (mode + filters + shaper + rate + volume) for one-click recall. One for classical, one for jazz, one for late-night listening. Export/import supported for backup and sharing.
  • Auto-match rules — bind a keyword to a preset; when the album title / artist / path matches, the preset applies itself. E.g. keyword "jazz" → your jazz preset follows automatically.

8. Phone remote

Click Phone Remote and scan the QR code: play, skip, volume and DSP presets from any phone browser — no app to install. The phone talks directly to the player over your LAN, so your Windows PC can be switched off afterwards.

9. NAA bridge

If your DAC hangs off a second device (NAA), the NAA Bridge page provides an independent SSH channel: connection test, shutdown, reboot and custom commands, with credentials stored separately from the player. Playback control itself doesn't need this page — it's a convenience for tinkerers.

11. FAQ

ssh: handshake failed: EOF on deploy or mount?

The player's SSH service (dropbear) rate-limits rapid repeated connections. Wait 1–2 minutes and retry — it is not a wrong password.

Can't connect to the player?

① Confirm the PC and player are on the same LAN. ② Use Scan — the IP may have changed. ③ Re-run Deploy to make sure hqpd is installed and running.

NAS mount fails?

Click Test first. Port unreachable → check IP/firewall. Port reachable but mount fails → check the log: nine times out of ten it's a typo in the share path or credentials. For ancient NAS boxes, switch the protocol to SMB 2.0 or 1.0.

Garbled CUE titles?

Shouldn't happen. Worldwide charset auto-detection is built in — GBK, Big5, Shift-JIS, EUC-KR, UTF-8 and Windows-125x are all handled.

Stuttering, or the Clips counter climbing?

Stuttering: engine overload — lower the rate or pick a lighter filter. Clipping: too much gain in the DSP chain — pull volume down 1–2 dB.

Reinstalling the OS — do I need a new license?

No. The license binds to the hardware (board + NIC). Re-import your original license.lic after reinstalling. For a new machine, contact the seller.

Does it collect any data?

No. There is no telemetry of any kind. License verification is offline, and all configuration lives on your own PC and player.
